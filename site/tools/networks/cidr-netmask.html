Prefix reference

CIDR vs netmask

A CIDR prefix such as /24 is shorthand for the number of leading 1 bits in a subnet mask. /24 equals 255.255.255.0 and leaves 8 host bits, i.e. 256 addresses. The wildcard (inverse) mask used by some ACLs is the bitwise NOT of the subnet mask.
