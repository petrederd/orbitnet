About this tool

This tool shows yourpublic IP address — the address that websites and online services see when you connect to them. This is different from your local/private IP address used within your home or office network.

Information displayed

  • IP Address: Your public IPv4 or IPv6 address
  • Location: Approximate geographical location (city, region, country)
  • ISP: Your Internet Service Provider
  • Organization: The organization that owns the IP block
  • Timezone: Your timezone based on location

Privacy note

This tool uses free public IP geolocation APIs. The location shown is approximate and based on your ISP's registration, not your exact physical location. No data is stored or logged by OrbitNet.
