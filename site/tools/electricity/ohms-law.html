Understanding Ohm's Law

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance in an electrical circuit:

  • Voltage (V): Measured in Volts — the electrical potential difference that drives current
  • Current (I): Measured in Amperes — the flow of electrical charge through a conductor
  • Resistance (R): Measured in Ohms (Ω) — the opposition to current flow
  • Power (P): Measured in Watts — the rate of energy consumption or production

Common applications

  • Calculating current draw for circuit design
  • Determining resistor values for LEDs
  • Sizing power supplies and batteries
  • Troubleshooting electrical circuits

Example calculation

If you have a12V power supply and a6Ω resistor:

• Current:I = V ÷ R = 12V ÷ 6Ω = 2A
• Power:P = V × I = 12V × 2A = 24W
