How it works

For numbers below 264, primality is decided exactly with a deterministic Miller–Rabin test. Larger inputs are checked with a probabilistic Miller–Rabin test (32 random rounds), so they are reported as probably prime rather than guaranteed. The input is limited to 200 digits. Factorisation uses trial division with a time limit, and the nearest-prime search is capped, so the page stays responsive on difficult inputs.
