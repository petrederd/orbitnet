Formulas

  • Mean — the arithmetic average (sum ÷ count)
  • Median — the middle value of the sorted data
  • Variance — population divides by n, sample divides by n − 1
  • Standard deviation — the square root of the variance
  • Quartiles — median of the lower and upper halves (Q1, Q3); IQR = Q3 − Q1
