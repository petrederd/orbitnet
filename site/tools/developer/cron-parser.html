Cron Expression Parser

Explain a five-field cron expression in plain English and see the next scheduled runs in your local time zone. Everything is calculated in your browser.

Schedule

minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week. Supports *, lists, ranges, steps and month/weekday names.

Result

Enter a cron expression to see its schedule.

Cron field order

minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week 0-59 0-23 1-31 1-12 0-7 (0 and 7 = Sunday)

Supported syntax: *, 5, 1-10, */5, 1-10/2, comma lists, three-letter month names (JAN–DEC) and weekday names (SUN–SAT). ? is treated as *.

When both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted, either field matching is enough (classic Vixie cron). A field beginning with *, including */2, counts as unrestricted for this rule. Quartz and some other cron implementations interpret these combinations differently.

Next runs are searched for up to eight years, which covers leap-day schedules even across skipped century leap years. The preview uses your browser's local time zone.
