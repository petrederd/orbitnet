Color Converter

Convert colors between HEX, RGB and HSL. Pick a color or type a value in any of the supported formats.

Input

Accepts #rgb, #rrggbb, rgb(...) and hsl(...).

Formats

Enter a color to convert.

Color models

  • HEX — a base-16 representation of the red, green and blue channels (00–FF each).
  • RGB — the three channels on a 0–255 scale.
  • HSL — hue (0–360°), saturation and lightness (0–100%), closer to how people describe colors.
