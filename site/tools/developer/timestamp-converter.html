Seconds or milliseconds?

Unix time counts seconds since 1 January 1970 UTC. Many languages and tools use milliseconds instead, which is 1000× larger. Auto-detect treats numbers with 10 or fewer digits as seconds and larger numbers as milliseconds. ISO 8601 strings such as 2026-01-01T12:00:00Z are parsed directly.
